Mantas is a 10-year open water lifeguard veteran who taught underprivileged kids surfing, kayaking and snorkeling for six of those summers. Then he moved to New York City to teach indoor cycling to overprivileged adults at SoulCycle for the next 10 years, helped open studios as far away as London, and later headed the launch of Commando in Mexico City. He started SurfYogaBeer in 2014 in the middle of his SoulCycle career and has grown the leaders and the crew in New York City, London, Mexico City and now Tampa Bay, Florida.

How did SYB start?
People from my SoulCycle class in New York wanted to keep hanging out after class. We went to Costa Rica together, everyone said “you have to do this again,” and it grew from there, people telling people.
Favorite SYB moment?
The last night of a trip, when a table of people who were strangers a week ago starts planning the next one together.
Something people wouldn’t guess about you?
I was an open water lifeguard for 10 years before I ever taught a spin class.
