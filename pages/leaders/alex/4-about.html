On the SYB squad: Senior Leader

How did you find SYB?
I came on my first SYB trip from London and ended up leading the next one. Many trips around the globe later, I’m still here.
Favorite SYB moment?
Bringing guests who are perhaps a little nervous, hiding in the background of the early group photos, to life. Immersing new guests in our community and getting them feeling as though they can be their true self among new friends is what it’s all about. Making memories, sweating and sinking a few beers at sunset in cool places is the cherry on top too, obviously.
Something people wouldn’t guess about you?
I might look like a jock, but I’m really a gentle giant.
Your go-to travel hack?
You don’t need to pack as much as you think, especially when the SYB swag is so fire. Pack lighter. You’ll realize you don’t need half of it.
