Sara is the wordsmith behind SurfYogaBeer’s blog, turning SYB adventures into stories you can feel. She captures the magic, the energy and the moments you wish you could relive, then leaves her mark the same way she does on every trip: bringing the crew together for unforgettable moments, great stories, and glitter you’ll be finding long after the trip is over.
