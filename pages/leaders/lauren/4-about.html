Meet Lauren.

From her first trip in 2023, Lauren puts the disco in San Francisco, bringing big energy, warmth, and that extra push you didn’t know you needed. A true mountain soul, dedicated yogi, and always first to rally the crew, she’s here to make your week unforgettable.

On the SYB squad: Wild Heart

How did you find SYB?
I wanted to visit somewhere new in the world that I knew nothing about and didn’t want to go alone, so I went to Morocco ’23 and was hooked. I went on a few more trips as a guest, then graduated SYB Academy in 2024!
Favorite SYB moment?
Dancing with new friends under the stars in the Philippines. You’re in the middle of an isolated island, so they’re as bright as can be.
Something people wouldn’t guess about you?
I taught myself how to DJ (badly) spending New Year’s Eve with my brother in our parents’ basement.
Your go-to travel hack?
Use the Flighty app. It keeps your gate information in one place even when your trip spans different airlines, makes it easy to share your flights with friends or family, and shows you a fun recap of everywhere you went at the end of the year.

  • Yoga
