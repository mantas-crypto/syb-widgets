    GOOD TO KNOW

    Questions teamsalways ask.

    Is this team building or a vacation?

    Both, on purpose. Every retreat mixes wellness, working sessions, long dinners, activities and quiet time, all done together. That's where the team building actually happens.

    How is an Ontario retreat priced?

    Sarah quotes each one. Pick your goals, group size, dates and what to include, send it to her, and she comes back with a price. Nothing is booked until you say go.

    Is Nicaragua safe?

    Yes. The villas are in a gated community with security on site 24 hours a day, and every transfer and activity is organized for you. SYB has run trips at Playa Maderas since 2016 with the same local team, drivers and surf coaches.

    Does everyone have to surf in Nicaragua?

    No. Surf lessons are there for anyone who wants them, and beginners start in the whitewater on big, stable boards. The pool, a hammock and a massage are fine answers too.

    Can people work during the trip?

    Yes. Add a workshop to an Ontario retreat. In Nicaragua the villas have fast fiber wifi, usually 100 Mbps or more, with a backup generator, and the work sessions setup in step 6 covers breakouts and whiteboarding.

    Which airport for Nicaragua?

    Managua, Nicaragua (MGA) is about 2.5 hours by road. Liberia, Costa Rica (LIR) is about 3 hours plus a land border crossing. Pickups and drop-offs at both are included. Land by 2pm on day one and fly home after 12pm on the last day.

    What if someone's flight doesn't line up?

    In Nicaragua, we set up a private driver for them. It usually runs $125 to $200 per car, fits up to three people with luggage, and is paid by the traveler.

    Do we need visas for Nicaragua?

    US citizens do not need a visa for a short stay. They pay a small tourist card fee in cash when they arrive. Other passports should check their own rules. Everyone needs a booked flight out of the country to show at the airport.

    Can we go somewhere else?

    Yes. Pick Custom in step 2, tell us where, how many and what you want, and Sarah builds it with you.
